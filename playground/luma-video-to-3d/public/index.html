Video → orbit (Luma Ray-2)

Luma's public Dream Machine API does not return a 3D mesh. The closest thing supported today is to take a frame from your video and ask Ray-2 to orbit around the subject — which gives a 3D-feeling rendered view. If you have a .glb exported from Luma Genie, you can drop it in the 3D viewer at the bottom.
